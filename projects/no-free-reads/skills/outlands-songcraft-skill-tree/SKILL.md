# Outlands Songcraft | Skill Tree v0.1

## Invocation
Read skill-tree/skills.json, track-development-matrix.json, learning-ledger.json and calibration-workbook.md from this project's root. Use for songwriting, critique, track revision, full-song generation, beat-arrangement design or listening analysis. Moist remains manager and needs human approval for adoption.

## Sequence
PROVENANCE: exact user words, assistant draft, Gemini textual critique and listener-heard lines remain separately attributed. Do not silently rewrite a source.

SCENE: one credible loadout and team dynamic plus a problem with a decision and cost. Verify current mechanics rather than inventing a game state.

PROPOSE: choose the track's skill-tree focus and preserve the original. Develop a genuinely alternate lyric or arrangement only within the creator's desired scope. For full-song requests, develop actual lyrical sections.

PROBE LANGUAGE: test compression, metaphor, mishearing and delayed punchline. Grammar and rhymes serve meaning; a perfect rhyme is not mandatory. Show coaching through executed comms.

PROBE MOUTH: study stresses and breaths across bars, not a fixed syllable formula. Preserve variation in conversational, triplet and double-time pockets.

PROBE MUSIC: Moist's lurching half-time trap, crisp hats, sliding gritty 808, eerie minor plucks, dark Rhodes, legato brash croon and bubbling synth can create feint → stop → reveal. Use signature textures as accents, not wallpaper.

REFLECT: distinguish text predictions from actual audio, track controlled changes, preserve unexpectedly productive heard phrases, ask creator for decisive taste input.

HUMAN GATE: no automated score, AI preference or audience metric may silently authorize canon, persona merge or release.

## Specific experimental finding
Assistant draft: "What the other team can see for." Human heard: "What did the other team concede for?" Creator then envisioned the narrator busy weighing three teammates' approaches until looking up to find the enemy trio already wiped. This is *not literal surrender*. The apparently awkward phonetic line becomes a delayed comic reveal; test before canon adoption.

## Return
Provenance, exact scene, two or three selected skill nodes, lyric/production variant with explicit hypothesis, a single grounded test question, status.
