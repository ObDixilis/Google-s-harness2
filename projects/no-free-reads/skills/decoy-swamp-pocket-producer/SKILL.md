# Decoy Swamp Pocket Producer · Skill v0.1

Cross-link sonic experiments to the project-specific Outlands Songcraft Skill Tree, `../outlands-songcraft-skill-tree/SKILL.md`. Feints and drops must pay off a dramatic or semantic event.

## When to apply
Use when designing a new production treatment for an approved or proposed track, checking album coherence, or evaluating audio supplied by the creator. Do not assert an audio outcome from text alone.

## Musical grammar
- **Kick/snare:** heavy Southern half-time pulse with displaced kicks and an occasional full-bar choke; stop-start transitions should have a cause.
- **Hats:** crisp, clipped, tightly gated sixteenths and rolling pickup triplets; leave a lane for fast consonants.
- **808:** gritty, sliding and audible on small systems, but retain controlled sub and mix headroom.
- **Harmonic color:** eerie minor-key plucks, short dark Rhodes stabs, economical muted guitar phrases.
- **Signature FX:** swampy, bubbling synthesizer accents as punctuation or responses to witty ad-libs. Use sparingly: two memorable appearances can outperform a constant texture.
- **Lead:** North American English low-tenor; conversational brash rap shifts into low, legato croon on emotional pivots; sharply timed crisp ad-libs.
- **Mix:** polished but tactile/gritty. Close dry lead; tight selective refrain doubles; controlled saturation, no excessive reverb.

## Feint → silence → answer
Create one purposeful *decoy* expectation: a predicted downbeat, apparent fill, or unresolved melodic pickup. Pull one or two rhythmic elements back briefly. Answer with an unexpected bass entrance, bar-ending lyrical reveal, or confident croon. Ensure dancers/listeners can still follow the pulse.

## Three treatment types
1. **Mudslide swagger:** sparse dark plucks, long sliding 808, hard stop-start syncopation; for NO FREE READS.
2. **Bogwater bounce:** bubbling synth/ad-lib call and response, slapstick silence, playful choked hats; for FULL WHERE?.
3. **Undertow restraint (production color, NOT identity merger):** soften 808 grit and synth density, permit warm Rhodes and sustained legato; for STATIC BETWEEN ROUNDS.

## Mandatory human gate
Input: track name, intended perspective, protected lyrics, creative phase, reference audio provenance and user constraints.
Output: three distinguishable Style-field candidates, Exclude field, section-level drop and vocal behavior, one precise listening question.
Never auto-select; await listening feedback. Keep phase orthogonal to runtime state.

## Suno quality checks
- Can fast consonants be understood? If not, adjust hat density and syllable attack before raising tempo.
- Does the croon deliver emotional consequence rather than genre decoration?
- Does the drop have a narrative or humor function?
- Does the swamp synth actually read as punctuation, or mask vocals?
- Did the render preserve the requested accent?
- Record generated / listened / inferred distinctly and never invent measurements.
